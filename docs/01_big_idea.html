Two Rules That Run Everything

①

c() — The Propagation Rule

c() takes any configuration and sends it to the next configurations, using complex numbers (numbers with magnitude AND direction). This creates interference — the heart of quantum mechanics.

②

t() — The Transformation Rule

t() transforms each configuration. Critically, it's bijective — every input maps to a unique output and vice versa. Nothing is lost. This is why information is conserved and time runs forward.

The Fixed-Point Equation

Here's the most important equation in the whole theory:

v₀ = T̂ · Ĉ(v₀)

The Big Bang seed (v₀) is the universe's own fixed point — it maps to itself under its own rules

🎯 Simple version — what is a fixed point?

Hold a mirror facing another mirror. You see reflections of reflections, going on forever. But look: the pattern in the middle reflects to itself. It's a fixed point — a pattern that maps to itself under the rule "reflect".

The equation v₀ = T̂·Ĉ(v₀) says: "the Big Bang seed is the mirror pattern" — the unique configuration that, when you apply the universe's two rules to it, you get back the same configuration. That's why the universe started the way it did.

What does this mean? The universe didn't start from arbitrary initial conditions. It started from the unique configuration that is self-consistent under the two rules. Like asking: what configuration of rules, when you apply those rules, gives you back the same configuration? There's only one answer — and that answer is the Big Bang seed.

💡 The universe exists the way a mathematical truth exists. The statement "2 + 2 = 4" doesn't need energy or a substrate to be true. It's true necessarily, by the internal consistency of arithmetic. Similarly, the CPT universe exists necessarily — it's the unique self-consistent execution of two rules.
