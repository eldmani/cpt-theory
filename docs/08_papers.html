Overview: What's Been Proved

🎯 Simple version — what "proof" means in math

In everyday speech, "proof" means strong evidence — like DNA evidence in a court case. In mathematics, proof means something much stronger: a logical chain where every single step follows necessarily from the previous one, with no gaps. A proved theorem is as certain as 1+1=2. It will never be overturned by a new experiment.

The two papers below prove mathematical facts conditional on the CPT axioms. If you accept the two axioms (c() is complex linear; t() is bijective), then the results follow with mathematical certainty — not as guesses or approximations, but as logical conclusions.

The two papers together prove that exactly 3 generations and 3 colors are required. They close the loop: from 2 axioms to the Standard Model structure.

Paper 09

Orbit-Color Identification and the Uniqueness of SU(3) as the Color Gauge Group

We prove that the color gauge group of the strong force must be SU(3) — not any other Lie group. The proof uses Cartan's complete classification of simple Lie groups, combined with the CPT orbit-color axiom (period-3 orbits carry color charge). The argument excludes all other candidates by dimension count.

Key Theorems

Thm 2.1
Orbit-color axiom: the Z₃ discrete symmetry of the period-3 orbit identifies with the center of the color gauge group G_c. Therefore center(G_c) ⊇ Z₃.
Status: ~ Conjecture (orbit-color identification is an input axiom, not derived)
Thm 3.1
Cartan classification result: Among all simple Lie groups with center containing Z₃, only SU(3) and E₆ exist.
Status: ✓ Theorem (established mathematical result)
Thm 3.2
SU(3) has fundamental representation of dimension 3. E₆ has fundamental representation of dimension 27.
Status: ✓ Theorem (Lie group theory)
Thm 4.1
The color-charge space has dimension 3 (three color states: R, G, B from the period-3 orbit). Therefore the fundamental representation of G_c has dim = 3. E₆ requires dim = 27 → E₆ is excluded. G_c = SU(3).
Status: ∂ Derived (given orbit-color axiom + Cartan)
Period-3 orbit → center Z₃ (orbit-color axiom)
Cartan: {G : center Z₃} = {SU(3), E₆}
Orbit has 3 states → dim(fund. rep) = 3 → SU(3) ✓ (E₆ has dim 27 ✗)

Master Rigour Classification

All claims in CPT are classified into four levels of rigour:

🎯 Simple version — the four levels

✓ Theorem: Rock-solid. Follows from axioms by established math. Like a proof in geometry class.

∂ Derived: Solid, but relies on one external result (like Noether's theorem or the Cartan classification) that CPT borrows from existing mathematics. Think of it as "proved, standing on another proved result."

~ Conjecture: Well-motivated guess. The argument is plausible and hasn't been disproved, but the proof has a gap. Scientists are still working on it.

→ Prediction: Something CPT says will be found in experiments. Not yet confirmed (or just showing early hints). This is the part that can be tested and potentially falsified.
